Context
The inheritance problem
Self-custody protects your assets from everyone — including your family, if something happens to you.

Today

No recovery path. Seed phrases are single-point-of-failure secrets. Hardware wallets become expensive paperweights. Sharing keys means losing security. Every year, billions in crypto become permanently unreachable — not stolen, just locked behind a missing passphrase.

With Thinkxx

Define a vault policy on-chain. Send heartbeats to prove you're alive. If your inactivity timer expires, a beneficiary can file a claim — but only after a grace period, and only with guardian quorum approval. No keys exposed. No trust assumptions. Just math and time.

Protocol
How it works
Four stages from setup to inheritance — all enforced on-chain.
01

Create Plan

Choose mode (Medical, Legal, Legacy). Set beneficiary, inactivity window, grace period.

02

Stay Active

Send heartbeats from the app. Each one resets the inactivity timer on your plan.

03

Claim Filed

If timer expires, beneficiary can initiate. Grace period starts. Owner can still cancel.

04

Guardian Vote

M-of-N guardians approve. After grace period + quorum → funds released to beneficiary.
